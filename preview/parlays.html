What these numbers mean

Different games assumes approximate independence. Mixed sports requires at least two sports. Same game uses experimental joint final-score scenarios, not multiplied leg probabilities. Experimental HR/TD props are supported across distinct games only. Same-game player dependence is not modeled.

Full-hit chance means every leg wins, excluding pushes. Full-win payout includes your stake. Expected return allows pushes to remove a leg at unchanged remaining odds; an all-push ticket refunds the stake. Confirm the book's rules, prices and ticket acceptance. The five-point stress lowers each leg's decided-bet chance by five percentage points; it is not a confidence interval. Multiple suggested tickets can share legs and are not independent of one another.

Different-game search uses at most 32 selections, with sport-balanced allocation in Mixed sports. Same-game search uses at most 12 selections per matchup. Different-game tickets support 2–11 legs. Same-game tickets support 2–3 game markets. Each search evaluates at most 150,000 complete combinations; different-game search also stops at 200,000 partial search steps, not an exhaustive market search. No account access, automatic wager, bankroll allocation, or guaranteed profit.
